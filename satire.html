This is satire

Last updated 3 November 2026

Neta Giri is a comedy game about politics. Please read it the way you would read a cartoon in the newspaper.

Characters are caricatures

Some characters, such as the party chief, the fixer, the prime-time anchor, the party rival and the tycoon, are exaggerated caricatures that poke fun at familiar types in public life. Their names are invented parodies.

Nothing a character says or does in the game is a claim about what any real person has said or done. The characters are fictional and the situations are made up.

Everything else is invented

Parties, companies, TV channels, newspapers, social media accounts and events in the game are fictional. Breaking-news events echo general patterns in the news, not specific facts about specific people.

Not an endorsement

The game lets you play a corrupt politician because satire works by exaggeration. It does not endorse corruption, violence, hate, or any party, ideology or leader. The developers have no affiliation with any political party, campaign, media house or company.

Concerns

If you think something in the game crosses a line, write to netagiri.simulator@gmail.com and it will be reviewed.
