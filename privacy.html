Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

Neta Giri is made by Advay Poddar and Advait Agarwal. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. Short version: your game stays on your phone, and the developers collect nothing about you.

What stays on your device

The app saves your game progress, the name you type for your character, your trophies and your settings (appearance, language, haptics) in the app's local storage on your device.

This data is never sent to the developers. It is deleted when you use Settings → Erase all data, or when you uninstall the app.

What the app downloads

When the app starts, it downloads a file of breaking-news events from a public web page hosted on GitHub Pages, a service run by GitHub, Inc. Like any website, GitHub receives standard technical information with that request, such as your IP address and the time of the request. The app does not send your name, your save game or any identifier with this request. GitHub handles that information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you are offline, the app uses the events that ship with it and nothing is sent.

What the developers do not do

No accounts or sign-in.

No analytics, tracking, advertising or advertising identifiers.

No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

No selling or sharing of personal information, because none is collected.

Your app store

Apple and Google process information when you download the app from their stores, under their own privacy policies. The developers may see aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as download counts and crash reports) that the stores provide.

Children

The game is political satire meant for teenagers and adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and no personal information is collected from anyone.

Your rights

Because the developers hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on their side. Your local data is under your control on your device. Questions are still welcome at netagiri.simulator@gmail.com.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting information (for example, crash reporting or online features), this policy will be updated before that happens, and the date at the top will change.
